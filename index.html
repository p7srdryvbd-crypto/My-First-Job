01 · ABOUT THE PROJECT

A simple idea for a real user need.

My First Job is designed for students and young people who are looking for their first work experience. The website provides a simple way to explore opportunities and simulate a job application.

01

The problem

Many young people do not know where to look for their first job or how to start an application.

02

The purpose

The purpose of this website is to make the first job search simple, clear and accessible.

03

The users

The target audience is students and young people with little or no professional experience.

04 · DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

From an idea to a working website.

I followed a simple development process to plan, build and test My First Job.

01

First — I planned

I chose the idea, defined the target audience and planned the six sections of the website.

02

Then — I designed

I created the layout, navigation menu, colors, cards and visual identity.

03

After that — I coded

I used HTML for the structure, CSS for the design and JavaScript for the interactive features.

04

Finally — I tested
